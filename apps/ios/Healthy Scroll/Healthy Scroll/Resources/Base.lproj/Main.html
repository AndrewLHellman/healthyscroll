Healthy Scroll

Skips the Reels you told it you don't want to see.

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions and turn on Healthy Scroll.
  2. Set it to Allow on instagram.com and healthyscroll.net.
  3. Sign in and write what you want skipped.
  4. Scroll Instagram Reels in Safari.
